Data & methods

School Education — EMRS (NESTS)
  • Sources. NESTS Annual Reports 2020-21, 2021-22, 2022-23 and 2023-24 (National Education Society for Tribal Students, Ministry of Tribal Affairs). The EMRS Guidelines (13 Nov 2020) and the NESTS Annual Calendar 2026 were inspected but contain no State/UT data.
  • What is counted. “Functional” and “sanctioned” EMRSs are different measures and are never combined. Construction status describes school buildings (completed / under construction / yet to start) and is not the same as a school being functional.
  • Reference periods. Each value is labelled with the annual report (financial year) it comes from. The 2023-24 values are “till 31 March 2024”.
  • Known source inconsistencies. In 2023-24 the state rows of the construction table add to 694 sanctioned schools while the printed total is 699; in 2022-23 the Tamil Nadu total columns disagree with its own components; Himachal Pradesh 2022-23 lists more functional (6) than sanctioned (4) schools. All are shown as printed and logged.
  • School locations. The reports contain no school identifiers or coordinates, so no school-point layer is drawn. Named schools are listed only where a report names them (buildings completed, foundation stones, inaugurations).
Higher Education — AISHE
  • Sources. AISHE State/UT tables for 2019-20 to 2023-24 (Ministry of Education), Tables 14, 19, 20, 38 and 1(a), extracted twice by independent pipelines with 0 differences across 8,928 cells.
  • Enrolment is estimated enrolment in institutions located in the State/UT — not students’ home state.
  • ST share = ST enrolment ÷ total enrolment (same table, same year). It is not the ST share of population.
  • GER uses Census-2011-based population projections from 2020-21; 2019-20 used an older projection (all-India GER 27.1 vs 25.6 on the 2011 basis), so 2019-20 GER/GPI is excluded from maps and trends. Where a State/UT has no ST population, ST GER/GPI is “not applicable”, never 0.
  • Not available. District- or institution-level ST enrolment is not published in these tables and is not estimated here.
Map, colours and missing values
  • Boundaries: DataMeet India community, States/Admin2 (36 States/UTs after January 2020; J&K and Ladakh updated per the Survey of India map, 2021), CC BY 4.0, simplified for display. Very small UTs are also marked with a dot so they can be selected.
  • Classes use fixed, documented breaks per indicator that do not change between years, so colours are comparable over time within one indicator.
  • Hatched areas have no record in the source table; they are not zeros. A printed 0 is shown in the lightest class.
  • The two sections are separate statistical systems; EMRS school counts and AISHE institution counts are never added or ranked together.
